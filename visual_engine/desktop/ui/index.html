Pyxel Canvas

100 generative 2D & 3D patterns, from fractals and nature simulations to abstract art, retro games, 3D sculptures and physics, rendered live on your PC.

Features

100 patterns

Six categories, each pattern with its own live controls.

Your look

8 colour palettes and 3 render resolutions.

Export

Save any render as PNG; export the 10 simulations as animated GIFs.

Learn how it works

Every pattern comes with notes explaining its mathematics and code.

Real science

Hydrogen orbitals, fluid dynamics, quantum tunnelling, flocking and more.

Offline & private

Everything runs on your computer. No account, no internet needed.

How to use

  1. Pick a category and a pattern from the list on the left.
  2. Adjust the palette, resolution and the pattern's own sliders.
  3. Click Render. The image appears on the left with its notes on the right.
  4. Click Export PNG or, for simulations, Export GIF. Files are saved to Pictures\Pyxel.
