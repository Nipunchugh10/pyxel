PYXEL CANVAS {version}
==================

100 generative 2D & 3D patterns: fractals, nature, abstract art, games,
3D sculptures and scientific simulations, rendered live on your PC.


HOW TO RUN
----------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example, your Desktop or Documents).
2. Open the folder and double-click  Pyxel.exe
3. Pick a category and a pattern on the left, adjust the sliders, and
   click Render. The image appears on the left with its notes on the right.

Keep Pyxel.exe next to the "_internal" folder; it needs the files in it.
Nothing is installed, and you need no Python or internet connection.


WINDOWS MAY WARN YOU
--------------------
The app is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say
"Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
If Smart App Control is on, Windows may block the app entirely.

If Pyxel says it "could not start", Windows may have blocked the files you
downloaded: delete the unzipped folder, right-click the zip > Properties >
tick "Unblock" > OK, and unzip it again.


REQUIREMENTS
------------
- 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
- Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime. It is part of Windows 11 and up-to-date
  Windows 10. If Pyxel says it is missing, install the free "Evergreen
  Runtime" from https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/


YOUR FILES
----------
Exported PNGs and GIFs are saved to your Pictures folder, in Pictures\Pyxel.
The app's "Open folder" link opens it.


UNINSTALL
---------
Delete the unzipped folder. Optionally delete Pictures\Pyxel.
